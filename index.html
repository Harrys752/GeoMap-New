About GeoMap Indonesia 2.0

Mission

GeoMap Indonesia 2.0 is an educational platform for exploring Indonesia's geological history, geological processes, paleontological heritage, and historical geohazards through an interactive map.

Vision

Making Indonesia's geological and paleontological heritage more accessible, understandable, and connected to spatial and environmental context.

Educational Goals

  • Introduce the diversity of Indonesia's geology.
  • Connect locations to the processes that formed them.
  • Introduce Indonesia's paleontological heritage.
  • Build basic understanding of geohazards.
  • Encourage map- and data-based geoscience literacy.

Data Confidence & Transparency Summary

Scientific source verification for GeoMap dataset entries is an active, transparent process. Statuses reflect internal audit verification stages rather than third-party agency certifications.

Project Scope & Methodological Limitations

  • No Live Feeds: Not a live seismic or volcanic monitoring system.
  • No Warning Alerts: Not a disaster warning system or emergency dispatch tool.
  • Non-Official: Not a replacement for official government channels (PVMBG / BMKG / BNPB).
  • Data Status: Data entries are historical, illustrative, or demo compilations.
  • Coverage: Data coverage is actively growing under structured scientific audit.
